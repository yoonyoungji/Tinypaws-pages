TinyPaws 1.4.5

This update improves edge walking, support prompts, and settings usability.

  • Improved edge walking so characters align more naturally with display boundaries and stay out of the way of clicks.
  • Added clearer, non-disruptive ways to support continued TinyPaws development.
  • Expanded the settings layout to prevent localized labels from being truncated.
  • Refreshed Russian Blue character artwork across movement and idle poses.

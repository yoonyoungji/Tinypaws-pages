TinyPaws 1.4.8

This update welcomes a mysterious new friend and improves support prompts near the top of the display.

  • Added a mysterious new character with dedicated idle, walking, running, jumping, and yawning animations.
  • Kept item drawing and sunglasses unavailable for this character so its original design stays intact.
  • Support prompts now appear below characters walking along the top edge, keeping the message fully visible.
